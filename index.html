Gates before every bid

  • Registration. A name on the released list is not proof of auction registration.
  • Medical and workload. Mandatory for all fast bowlers, a hard gate for Pathirana.
  • Role. Name the batting slot or overs window first. No second player for the same role.
  • Price. The index is scouting evidence, not a valuation. Keep purse for six slots.

Limitations

  • Pressure is a state-based proxy, not a measure of psychology.
  • Ranks are cohort-relative, not fully adjusted for position, venue, opposition or pace versus spin.
  • Several top names have T20I-only samples. Small samples (100 to 150 balls) drive several bowler cases.
  • Prices, injuries and registration come from public announcements and must be rechecked on auction day.
